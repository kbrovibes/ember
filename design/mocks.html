Recommendation

Ship Tape, with Mono's rolling numerals as its time display.

  • Drag is the product. A ruler you pull under a fixed needle is the most physical way to set time, and it carries Red Hot Timer's gesture without copying its look. Each minute is a detent, so it maps to trackpad haptics.
  • One idea, three sizes. The same tape works at 288 pt in the popover, 520 pt in the window and edge to edge in fullscreen. Rings and vessels need a fixed square.
  • Quiet at rest. Neutral ticks, a single ember needle, and a thin progress bar. Accent appears only where time is.
  • Trade-off. A tape is less glanceable than a ring from across a room. The menu-bar mini tick and fullscreen numerals cover that.
